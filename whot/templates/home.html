How to play

Match the top card by shape or number. Can't play? Tap the market to pick one card. First to finish all cards wins. If the market runs out, the fewest cards wins.

  • 1 Hold on: play again
  • 2 Pick two: next player picks 2, or defends with a 2
  • 5 Pick three: next player picks 3, or defends with a 5
  • 8 Suspension: next player misses a turn
  • 14 General market: everyone else picks 1, you play again
  • 20 Whot: play anytime and choose the shape
